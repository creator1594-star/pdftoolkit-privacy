Privacy Policy — PDF Toolkit

Effective date: October 7, 2026
Applies to: PDF Toolkit (Android), by CreatorSprout
Contact: hello@creatorsprout.com

The short version

PDF Toolkit processes everything on your device. We collect no files, no personal data, no analytics. Your documents never leave your phone — there is nothing to sell, share, or leak, because we never receive it.

What the app does on your device

  • Merges, splits, compresses, and converts PDF files entirely on your phone — no uploads, no cloud processing.
  • Reads files you pick through the Android file picker and saves results to your device's storage. Originals are never overwritten.
  • App preferences and the list of recently produced files are stored only on your device.
  • The app requests no internet permission and includes no analytics or advertising SDKs.

What we collect

Nothing. PDF Toolkit:

  • requires no account and no sign-in;
  • sends no analytics, crash reports, advertising identifiers, or device data to us or any third party;
  • shows no third-party ads;
  • does not access your contacts, location, microphone, camera, or messages.

PDF Toolkit Pro (in-app purchases)

PDF Toolkit is free: 5 full operations per day at full quality, with no watermarks. Viewing PDFs is always free and unlimited. PDF Toolkit Pro unlocks unlimited operations:

  • $4.99/month — includes a 1-week free trial
  • $29.99/year — includes a 1-week free trial
  • $14.99 one-time — lifetime access, no renewal

All purchases are processed by Google Play under the Google Play Terms of Service — we never see your card information.

Children

PDF Toolkit collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date. The app itself will not change how it handles your data without an app update you choose to install.
